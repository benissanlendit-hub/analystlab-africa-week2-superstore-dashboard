# analystlab-africa-week2-superstore-dashboard
Business Intelligence dashboard - Superstore Sales Analysis - AnalystLab Africa Week 2
🎯 Objective

As a Junior Business Intelligence Analyst, this project delivers an interactive executive dashboard for a national retail company's management team to monitor sales performance, profitability, customer behavior, and regional performance — turning raw transactional data into actionable, data-driven decisions.

Built as part of Week 2 of the AnalystLab Africa Data Analytics Internship Programme.

📊 Dataset

Superstore Sales Dataset (Kaggle) — 9,994 order line items from 2014 to 2017, covering:

3 customer segments (Consumer, Corporate, Home Office)
3 product categories (Technology, Furniture, Office Supplies) across 17 sub-categories
4 US regions (West, East, Central, South)
🛠️ Tools Used
Microsoft Power BI Desktop — data modeling, DAX measures, interactive dashboard
Power Query — data cleaning, type correction, and transformation
DAX — calculated columns and measures (Total Sales, Total Profit, Profit Margin, Average Sale, Order Year/Month)
💡 Key Insights
Sales grew 51% from 2014 ($484K) to 2017 ($733K), with profit margin improving from ~10.2% to ~12.7%
The West region leads on both sales and profit; the Central region has comparable sales volume but the weakest margin
Technology is the most profitable category, while Furniture — despite similar revenue — is dragged down by the structurally unprofitable Tables sub-category
Profit correlates negatively with discount level (r = -0.22): heavier discounting is closely tied to lower profitability
Sales are highly seasonal, peaking every November–December
The Consumer segment drives over half (51%) of total sales
